## 2. Every placeholder-generated lesson

They were not 57 separate files. They were **one function**, `autoDeck()` —
formerly at `lib/notebook.ts:3910`, **now deleted** — invoked by `buildDeck()`
for any `(subject, level)` not present in `FLAGSHIPS`. The generated body read:

> *"In this unit we explore "{unit}". Ask the tutor for a worked example set in
> {subject}, and try the lab and quiz below to lock it in."*

This was the single highest-priority content deletion in the project. It:

- contained the forbidden phrase **"ask the tutor for"** (DO NOT #2);
- outsourced teaching to a tutor the lesson had not scoped;
- produced 57 lessons that passed every automated check because they were
  grammatical, safe and age-appropriate — and taught nothing. This is precisely
  the gap `creative-director` exists to close.

**Done.** The function is gone and the 57 now show an honest state.

The 43 hand-authored decks are **not** placeholders and are genuine work, but
none of them meets the lesson schema either: none carries standards,
misconceptions, engine bindings, an artifact, tutorScope, provenance, or a11y
paths. They are a good raw source for `lesson-author`, not lessons.

Separately, three hand-authored decks contain the softer phrase *"Try it, then
ask the tutor to check your steps."* at `lib/notebook.ts:97`, `:160`, `:486`.
That is closer to legitimate — the child acts first — but it still routes the
teaching to the tutor and should be reviewed when those decks are converted.

**That is roughly one item per lesson where 12 are required — about 8% of the

L8), and math has no static bank at all.

> The README claims math and upper physics are "procedurally generated, so they
> never repeat". That is true of the generators, and it is why those banks are
> empty — but generated items are not reviewable, cannot be tagged to a
> standard, and cannot have distractors mapped to named misconceptions. For B2B
> they do not count as assessment items. Treat math and physics 6–10 as **0
> reviewable items**, not as covered.
