## What we are

The Aigency is a creative solutions AI studio based in Abu Dhabi, UAE. We sit
between a creative agency and a product studio: we use AI as the production
method rather than as the subject matter, which means we can take a brief from
concept to finished, on-brand output at a speed and unit cost a traditional
studio cannot match.

## What we sell

- **AI-native content production** — campaign creative, film and video, stills,
  brand systems, and localisation across Arabic and English.
- **Applied AI product work** — internal tools, agentic workflows, and
  customer-facing AI features, from prototype to deployed.
- **Creative operations and enablement** — pipelines, brand-locked templates and
  training so a client's own team can produce at volume without losing
  consistency.
- **Strategy and advisory** — where AI genuinely changes a client's cost,
  speed or reach, and where it does not.

## Where we work

Primary market: UAE (Abu Dhabi and Dubai). Secondary: wider GCC — Saudi
Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman. We work in Arabic and English and treat
Arabic-first output as a first-class deliverable, not a translation step.

## How we are different

- AI-native production, not AI-assisted retrofitting of an old pipeline.
- Bilingual and culturally fluent for the region, including right-to-left
  design done properly.
- We ship working things, not decks about shipping things.
- Small senior team, no layered account management between the client and the
  people doing the work.

## Voice

Direct, warm, specific. We write like practitioners talking to practitioners:
concrete nouns, real numbers, no hype vocabulary, no "revolutionise", no
"unlock the power of". We would rather show one made thing than list ten
capabilities. Short sentences carry the weight. Arabic copy is written in
Arabic, not translated from English.

## Lines we do not cross

- No claim we cannot evidence — no invented metrics, clients or credentials.
- No fabricated personalisation in outreach.
- Business contact data from public professional sources only.
- Every commercial message carries a real opt-out and honest sender identity.
